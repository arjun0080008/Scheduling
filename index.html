Rule cards — how this tool decides (plain English)

Who is in clinic

Every resident has one rotation per block. The letter tells you the clinic pattern:

  • O — outpatient block. In clinic every weekday of that block.
  • N — inpatient, night float or ICU. No clinic at all that block.
  • E — anything else (elective, SNF, ED Obs, community). One fixed weekday each week — set it in Settings.
  • A — away rotation. Treated like E, but shown as "away — confirm". Always ring to confirm.

A resident on their elective day gets the full template for that weekday, exactly like an outpatient resident.

Slot classes

  • Est (blue) — follow-ups. Bookable any time.
  • New (teal) — new patients only until 14 days before the date; after that it becomes an Est slot.
  • FROZEN (red outline) — hidden until 2 days before. Then released for urgent, hospital discharge or waitlist only.
  • HospFU (amber) — hospital follow-up only, within 14 days of discharge.
  • Any (purple) — PGY-1 slots. Anything, always 60 minutes.

The numbers

  • Target per resident per day: PGY-2 12 / 6 (Thu) / 10 (Fri) · PGY-1 5 / 3 / 4.
  • Pre-book ceiling = 70% of target, rounded down. Advance bookings may never go above it. The held-back 30% is Frozen, hospital follow-ups and flex.
  • Supervision cap = attendings × 32 (× 16 on Thursday, morning only).
  • Day total = the smaller of (all targets added up) and (supervision cap).
  • Hard maximum per resident = the number of slots in the template. Never go above it. Never shorten a slot.

Continuity levels

  • Must see own doctor — never moved to anybody else.
  • Prefer own team — may move to the firm partner only.
  • Any doctor — may move freely.

A firm is one PGY-2 and one PGY-1 paired together, sitting in one attending's lane.

When somebody calls out

  • Must see own doctor → the firm partner takes them, up to 2 over target. Anyone left goes to that doctor's next clinic date.
  • Prefer own team → firm partner first, then anyone else in the same lane.
  • Any doctor → any open slot today.
  • Whoever is left goes on the nurse reschedule list.

Edge cases worth knowing

  • Thursday is morning only — 8 slots for PGY-2, 4 for PGY-1, and the supervision cap halves.
  • Friday starts at 9:00 — no 8:00 or 8:30.
  • A new patient no-show means the slot becomes an Est slot; fill it from the follow-up waitlist.
  • A graduating resident hands their panel to a named successor — change the roster in Settings at the end of June.
  • An away rotation always needs a phone call to confirm before you book.
  • Dates outside 1 July 2026 – 30 June 2027 are outside this year's block schedule.
